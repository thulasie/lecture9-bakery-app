Start the app

  1. Open Terminal in this project folder.
  2. Type python3 app.py and press Return. On Windows, use python app.py.
  3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 in your browser. Keep Terminal open while using the app.

To stop, press Control+C in Terminal. To return later, repeat the steps. Your decisions are saved automatically. If the address is already in use, close the other bakery server first.

Work through the week

  1. The app starts on Monday, November 23, 2026. The 13 earlier orders are already accepted. It imports all 25 messages from the seven Instagram files once, but shows them only as their planning day arrives.
  2. Read the earliest pending message. The eligibility explanation checks notice, matching orders, and the oven hours left on pickup day. Accept or reject it yourself. Eligible means the rules allow it; it is not a promise of maximum profit. Capacity is recalculated after every decision.
  3. For a capacity problem, book a helper if offered, then accept the request. Review the $100 fee first. You can reject any request, even an eligible one. Later messages wait until earlier messages are decided.
  4. Use the pickup book to find customers and mark orders collected on or after their pickup day. Collection does not remove their baking hours or sales.
  5. Use “Add a request” for additional messages received on the planning day. Changes and cancellations match the customer name (ignoring case) and original pickup date. Changes replace the whole product and quantity; they do not add quantities. Product and quantity fields are ignored for cancellations.
  6. Before “Finish day,” decide all pending messages and book any helpers you want. Advancing reveals the next day's messages and cannot be reversed. The supplied planning period ends December 1, including the later pickups. This is a teaching planner, not a clock that follows today's real date.

Rules and decisions

  • Cupcakes cost $40 per dozen and require 30 oven minutes per dozen. Cakes cost $120 each and require 2 oven hours each. Quantities are positive whole dozens or whole cakes, with one product per order.
  • Orders bake on pickup day, and all pickups are at 5 PM. Daily capacity is 8 oven hours. The app never accepts a request that exceeds booked capacity.
  • New orders, changes, cancellations, and helper bookings need at least 2 calendar days' notice: Monday → Wednesday is allowed. Message dates are used for customer notice, and the current planning day is used for helper notice. All messages must be decided before advancing, so decisions are made on their received day.
  • A helper costs $100 for one pickup day and adds 4 hours. Assumption: at most one helper per day. Bookings commit the fee and cannot be undone in this version. No helper is booked automatically.
  • Rejected changes and cancellations leave the original order intact. Accepted cancellations free its capacity and remove its sales. A missing or ambiguous matching order blocks approval. A rejected original order cannot be changed or cancelled.
  • Cakes commonly arrive a week ahead, cupcakes in the final 3 days, and Saturday is busiest. These are planning guidance, not extra notice rules or demand predictions. No future messages are used to decide today's requests.
  • Cupcakes generate $80 per oven hour and cakes $60. “After helper” means accepted sales minus the day's helper fee. It excludes ingredients, wages and other costs; it is not profit. There is no automatic sales optimization.

Implementation details

app.py serves the HTML, CSS, JavaScript and JSON API using Python's standard library HTTP server, with no packages, Node.js or AI. It listens only on this computer at port 8000. The app is intended for local use, not public hosting.

All persistent app data lives in bakery.db, beside the program: orders, imported requests and their source text, helper bookings, planning day, collection status and decision history. The CSV and seven bakery HTML pages are read on first initialization. They are never edited. Restarting does not duplicate imports; changes to source files after initialization are not reimported. Workflow Builder files are separate and unused.

The API exposes GET /api/state and POST /api/action. Server-side rules recheck every decision inside a SQLite write transaction; browser button states are only a convenience. SQL parameters and escaped page text protect customer input. Decisions are recorded with their planning day and reason.

To back up your work, stop the app and copy bakery.db somewhere safe. Do not delete it unless you intend to erase all decisions. This version does not provide undo, date changes, payments, inventory, customer messaging or external Instagram syncing. Rejected or cancelled records stay visible for reference.

Verification

Run python3 test_app.py to check imports, notice boundaries, cancellation and change handling, helper capacity and costs, chronological decisions, persistence and the complete supplied week. Tests use temporary copies of the database and never change the real one.
